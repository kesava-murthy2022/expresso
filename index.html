How to play
  • Tiles sit face-down in the middle. Flip one on your turn, in front of everyone.
  • First find your colour start tile. Wrong tile? Flip it back, next player.
  • Then collect your character's emotions in order along your side. Keep flipping while you are right.
  • A miss flashes the screen red, the tile flips back by itself, and the next player goes. Remember where it was!
  • Expresso tile: grants one extra flip, then leaves the game.
