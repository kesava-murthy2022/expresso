How to play
  • Tiles sit face-down in the middle. Flip one on your turn.
  • First find your colour start tile.
  • Then collect your character's emotions in order along your side. Keep flipping while you are right.
  • A miss flashes red, the tile flips back, and the next player goes. Remember where it was!
  • Expresso tile: one extra flip, then it leaves the game.
